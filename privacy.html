Veda — Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026

In short

Veda has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don’t collect your data.

On your device

Your books, notes, chats and listening history stay on this device. Importing, reading scanned pages, narration, the book chat, search and backups all run here.

When Veda goes online

Only when you ask: browsing or downloading free books, importing a web page, fetching covers, or installing a voice. Each request goes to the site that serves it and carries only your search words or the item you chose — never your library.

Purchases

Apple handles buying and restoring. We never see your payment details.

Camera

Used only to photograph pages you turn into a book. The photos are read on this device.

Backups and sharing

They go only where you send them, such as iCloud Drive or a folder you pick.

Reports

If you report an issue, we receive what you write and, if you attach it, the diagnostic log, which you can read or delete in the app first.
